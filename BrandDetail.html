EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

La Sabia Madrid operates as an independent Spanish niche house in the contemporary artistic segment, with retail prices ranging from €150 to $165 per 100ml. The brand is positioned as a high-artistic, emerging house centered around 'The Artisan's Collection', combining Mediterranean freshness, gourmand-leather contrasts, and woody-spicy depth rooted in Spanish cultural identity. It demonstrates strong early momentum as a 2026 launch, driven by recognized perfumers like Suzy Le Helley and Philippine Courtière, and rapid social traction across key SKUs like Dos Besos and Poderío.

2. CONSOLIDATED RANKING

Applied Methodology

The ranking applies a 60% Artistic Score / 40% Commercial Score weighting. System A uses cumulative temporal windows (L12M / L24M / L36M) for momentum analysis, while System B uses non-overlapping periods (P1Y, P2Y, P3Y). As a 2026 launch, all SKUs are classified as new_release ⚡.

3. STRATEGIC BLOCKS

Mandatory Core

MANDATORY CORE (High Rotation & Traffic): Includes Poderío (by Suzy Le Helley) and Dos Besos (by Théo Belmas & Margherita Carini). These two SKUs represent an estimated 55–65% of total brand volume. Commercial insight: They hold the highest L12M mentions (25.1 and 29.5), strong longevity (9h), and combine viral traction with perfumer prestige for strong conversion. 🕊️

Profitable Complement

PROFITABLE COMPLEMENT: Includes Floreal (by Philippine Courtière) and Conquista (by Théo Belmas). These expand usage occasions to daytime and spring/summer, support cross‑selling, and maintain a strong artistic‑commercial balance (scores 81–83). 🥇

Selective

SELECTIVE (Image & Niche Depth): Includes Verdanzza (by Théo Belmas & Margherita Carini). Despite a lower Commercial Score (55) and low mentions (4.3/month), it maintains artistic credibility (78 Artistic Score) and is vital for collection completeness and brand.
